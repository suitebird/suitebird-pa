One resident, five seats, one number at the top

Every screen reads from the same dataset. When the social worker sends a handoff, the sales director receives it, and the recovered bed days that result are the same bed days the portfolio view counts. Nothing on the landlord screen is typed in.

The episode

The clock does not care which day it is. It watches whatever the authorisation says: the coinsurance cliff on day 21, the benefit exhausting at day 100, or an Advantage plan that stops authorising in week two. Same code, three residents.

    Without this, the only formal signal in that timeline is a notice issued 48 hours before coverage ends. Everything else is memory and a phone call.
