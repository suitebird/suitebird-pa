Structural vacancy and recoverable vacancy are different problems

A building below target because of market, unit mix, or condition is a real estate question. A building below target because beds sit dark between stays is an operations question, measured at daily resolution rather than quarterly average. The split below separates the two. Only the recoverable share is addressable here, and reporting it any other way would overstate what this can do.
