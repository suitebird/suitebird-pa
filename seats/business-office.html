Two clocks, not one

Traditional Part A runs a 100 day benefit you can count down, with coinsurance starting at day 21. Medicare Advantage runs no clock at all. It is a rolling authorization reviewed every five to seven days that can end with two days of notice at any point in the stay. The board draws them differently on purpose. A solid bar is a countdown. Dashed blocks are authorization windows that have not been granted yet.
